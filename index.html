GamePost

Text a game photo. It shows up on your club's Facebook and Instagram.

GamePost is a small service run by Antonio Vargas, a sole proprietor in Brookfield, IL. It helps youth sports clubs share game-day photos. A coach, parent, or team volunteer whom the club has approved texts a photo to the GamePost number, with an optional short caption. GamePost formats the photo (for example, adding the team name and the club's hashtags) and posts it to that club's own Facebook Page and Instagram account. GamePost posts only to the accounts of clubs that have authorized it, and only photos from numbers the club approved.

How it works

  1. Sign up below with the club or team you volunteer for. Check the optional SMS box if you want to send photos by text.
  2. I confirm with your club that you're an approved sender, then add your number. You'll get one text confirming you're set up.
  3. Text a photo (and an optional caption) to the GamePost number. You'll get a short reply saying whether it posted.

Texts you get from GamePost are only setup confirmations, replies about photos you sent, and posting status. GamePost never sends marketing or promotional texts.
