Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

GamePost is operated by Antonio Vargas, a sole proprietor in Brookfield, IL ("I," "me"). This policy covers this website, the GamePost sign-up form, and the GamePost text messaging program. It is the only privacy policy for GamePost.

Information I collect

  • Sign-up form: your name, email, mobile number, club and team, role, whether you checked the optional SMS consent box, and when you submitted.
  • Texts you send: your phone number, the photos you text, any caption you include, and the time.
  • Records: which club account a photo was posted to, and your SMS consent and opt-out history.

How I use it

Only to run GamePost: to confirm with your club that you're an approved sender, to format your photos and post them to your club's own Facebook Page and Instagram account, to text you confirmations and posting status, to answer questions, and to keep a record of your consent. I don't use your information for marketing, and I don't add you to any marketing list.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

I do not sell, rent, or trade your information. To run GamePost I use service providers that handle information only on my behalf, to deliver the service: Twilio (sends and receives text messages and photos), FormSubmit (delivers sign-up forms to my email), Google/Gmail (stores those emails), and Meta (Facebook and Instagram, where the photos you send are published to your club's accounts). Your club may be told that you signed up, so it can approve you as a sender. I may disclose information only if the law requires it.

Photos

  • Photos you text are posted only to the Facebook Page and Instagram account of the club you signed up with, and only after that club has authorized GamePost to post for it. Once posted, a photo is public on those accounts and is also subject to Meta's policies.
  • Your phone number is never included in a post.
  • Minors: youth sports photos often show children. By sending a photo, you confirm you have the right to share it and that posting it is allowed under your club's photo policy and by the families involved. Don't send photos that show a child's full name, address, school, or other personal details.
  • Removal: anyone (a parent, player, coach, or club) can ask for a photo to be taken down by emailing vargas.tony@gmail.com or calling 312-833-1313. I'll remove it from the club's pages and delete my copy, normally within 2 business days. The club can also remove posts from its own accounts at any time.
  • I keep stored copies of submitted photos for no more than 90 days, then delete them.

Text messaging

You opt in to GamePost texts only by checking the optional SMS consent box on the sign-up form at https://iamantonio.github.io/gamepost-site/ and submitting it. Messages are setup confirmations, replies about photos you submit, and posting status. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. See the Terms & SMS Terms.

Children

GamePost senders must be adults (18 or older). I don't knowingly collect sign-ups or texts from children.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any GamePost text to stop texts. Email vargas.tony@gmail.com to see, correct, or delete your information, or to have photos removed.

Changes

If I change this policy, I'll update the effective date above.
